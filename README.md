# 🚴 Adventure Works Bike Shop – Sales & Revenue Analysis (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes the **sales and revenue performance** of **Adventure Works Bike Shop** using **Power BI**.  
The dashboard provides insights into sales trends, product profitability, and customer purchasing behavior, enabling data-driven decisions for growth and profitability.  

---

## 📊 Key Objectives
- Track **total sales and revenue** over time  
- Identify **best-selling bike categories** (Mountain, Road, Touring)  
- Analyze **profitability by products** (Bikes, Accessories, Clothing)  
- Evaluate **top 100 customers by revenue and orders**  
- Study **customer demographics** – income level & occupation  
- Calculate **revenue per customer** and number of unique customers   

---

## 🔍 Analysis & Insights
### Sales & Revenue
- **Road Bikes** generate the highest sales volume,profit and revenue
- **Tires and Tubes** from **accessories** is the most sold subcategory 
- Accessories & Clothing contribute to repeat purchases
- **Shorts** has the highest return rate

### Customer Insights
- **Top 100 customers** drive a significant share of revenue  
- **Professionals** puts the highest no.of orders 
- Strong repeat business from bike owners purchasing **accessories/gear**  

---

## 📈 Power BI Visuals
The dashboard includes:  
- **Revenue Trends** (Line Chart)  
- **Orders by categories** (Bar Chart)  
- **Monthly orders,profit and revenue vs target of Products** (Guage)  
- **Top Customers** (Ranking Table & Bar Chart)  
- **Orders by Income & Occupation** (Donut chart)  
- **KPI Cards** (Revenue, Profit, Unique Customers)
- **Orders,Profit,Revenue,Returns,Return Rate of each category,subcatgories of Products** (Decomposition Tree) 

---

## 🚀 Recommendations
- Promote **high-margin Products**  
- Bundle **bikes with accessories** to increase average order value  
- Implement a **customer loyalty program** for top customers  
- Launch **targeted marketing campaigns** based on income & occupation  
- Optimize **inventory** for fast-selling mountain bikes and accessories  

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI** – Data visualization & dashboard creation  
- **Adventure Works Dataset** – Bike shop sales & customer data  
- **DAX** – Measures & calculated columns  
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning & transformation
